Ecological foundations

Simulation model & scientific limitations

Individual terrestrial vertebrates and pterosaurs have persistent IDs, positions, age, energy, hydration, health, stamina, fear and activity. Encounters depend on distance, habitat, wind, scent and perception. Fish and invertebrates are spatial cohorts rather than individually simulated. Terrain is a fixed synthetic topographic field shared by movement and map rendering. Weather, fire and decay are simplified ecological models, not palaeontological forecasts. The mixed-era assemblage is fictional. Daily updates are simulated as daily steps, not continuous hours. Not every death can be attributed to a single certain cause.
