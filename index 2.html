Ecological foundations

Simulation rules & limitations

One visible dot represents each animal in the census. Positions are persistent pseudo-individual visualisations of habitat cohorts, not individually simulated AI agents. Social clusters, schools and movement are visual models; population dynamics still operate by cohorts. Predation uses prey availability, predator demand and a daily success roll. Population growth uses food, water, habitat carrying capacity and reproductive intervals. Migration responds to crowding, scarcity and tidal access. Fires and drought arise from environmental probability rolls, not a scripted narrative.

This is a fictional mixed-era prehistoric ecosystem. Species ecology, carrying capacities and rates are explicit gameplay assumptions, not established palaeontological measurements. The model is an early ecological prototype, not a scientifically validated prediction.
